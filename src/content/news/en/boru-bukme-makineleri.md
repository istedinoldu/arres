**Tube bending machines** are essential manufacturing technologies for forming metal and industrial tubes into accurate geometries without wrinkling, flattening, or compromising wall integrity. They play a key role across automotive exhaust and chassis lines, furniture, HVAC, marine and defense applications, and general machinery manufacturing.

Modern **CNC tube bending** systems combine multi-axis control, programmable bend angles, and high repeatability to reduce scrap and improve assembly fit across both high-volume runs and custom batches. Mandrel tooling, multi-radius capabilities, and automation integration help achieve consistent form and surface quality even on tight bends and complex geometries.

**Arres Mühendislik Makine** supports industrial manufacturers in improving tube-forming efficiency and part quality through dedicated engineering expertise, custom machinery, and overhaul/modernization solutions.
