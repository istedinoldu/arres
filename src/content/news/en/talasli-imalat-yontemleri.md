**Machining methods** are foundational processing technologies with wide application across the metalworking industry. They are used to shape and machine a variety of metal components to tight specifications.

Machining encompasses operations such as **turning**, **milling**, **drilling**, and related processes. Turning is used for shaping rotational parts, whereas milling is effective for machining broader surfaces, and drilling is preferred for hole-making operations.

These methods play a critical role in bringing metal components to required dimensions and geometries. Through precision machining techniques and modern machine tool technologies, machining processes enable high-quality results.

Machining methods can be integrated with **CNC (Computer Numerical Control)** technologies to become more accurate and programmable. This facilitates customized part machining and adds flexibility to manufacturing operations.

In the metalworking industry, machining methods are applied across a broad spectrum of sectors. They serve a fundamental role in metal component production across automotive, aerospace, defense, machinery manufacturing, and many other industrial fields.

Overall, machining methods represent effective and versatile processing techniques widely used in metalworking, delivering quality, precision, and efficiency across industrial production workflows.
