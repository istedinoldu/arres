ARRES participated in the **CFE Carpet & Flooring Expo**, held between December 5 and 8, showcasing its pioneering innovations and products in the field of carpet equipment. Throughout the exhibition, the company attracted strong interest by introducing new products and engineering solutions that shape the industry.

While this year's CFE Carpet & Flooring Expo brought together leading names in the sector, ARRES stood out with its original, high-quality machinery and equipment. Visitors had the opportunity to explore the latest trends and technological advancements in carpet equipment through the wide product range displayed at our booth.

Commenting on the event, ARRES CEO Mr. Korhan stated:

> "It is a great pleasure for us to take part in such a prestigious event as the CFE Carpet & Flooring Expo. Here, we had the opportunity to meet industry professionals and prospective clients, showcase our products, and contribute to the sector. We aim to further strengthen our presence in the industry through our innovative solutions and quality."

With its customer-oriented approach and valuable contributions to the industry, ARRES secured a strong position at the CFE Carpet & Flooring Expo and earned the appreciation of visitors. Once again, the company demonstrated its strong footprint in the carpet equipment sector through advanced technology and a refined product portfolio.

Throughout the event, ARRES strengthened its industry network and laid solid groundwork for new collaborations and projects. The strong interest shown by visitors served as an even greater source of motivation for ARRES's upcoming projects and innovation initiatives.
