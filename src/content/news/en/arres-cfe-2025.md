**Arres Mühendislik Makine** met with industry professionals and visitors at **CFE Carpet & Flooring Expo**, held from **January 7 to 10, 2025**. Throughout the exhibition, the company presented engineering solutions, tufting and production equipment, and custom machinery capabilities tailored to the carpet and flooring industry.

Discussions held during the expo with domestic and international manufacturers created valuable touchpoints around production line efficiency, automation, and new business collaborations. **Arres Mühendislik** strengthened its standing in carpet equipment and industrial machinery through dedicated engineering and dependable technical support.
